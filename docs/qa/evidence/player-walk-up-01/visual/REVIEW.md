`own-actual-ABCD-1x.mp4` is a simultaneous, common-scale four-condition excerpt at **unaltered temporal rate1×**. `own-actual-ABCD-1x-enlarged.mp4` is only a spatial4×nearest enlargement. PC video downsampling1920→640 reduces tiny-foot certainty; still/native plates supplement it, not replace live motion. Chronological sampling can alias0.300s gait; this evidence does not establish every intervening pose naturalness. Preview/no-movement pose strips cannot prove locomotion.

Rear perspective means a near raised sole may project below a distant planted boot; image-bottom alpha is **not** a support-plane/physical-contact test. Phase-correlation torso offsets have weak response and preprocessing sensitivity, so they are appearance-registration estimates, not measured pelvis/centre-of-mass motion. No zero-slip, physically correct contact, Android device PASS, production adoption, or complete natural-gait PASS is claimed.

Recommendation: preserve trial C for director comparison, retain A/D controls, and request a clearly distinct right rear push-off candidate if the aim is the final two-leg-quality completion. Do not fabricate it by mirroring the whole character or changing existing adopted PNGs.
