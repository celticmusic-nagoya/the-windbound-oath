# Precision measurement and correction playbook (2026-11-22)
Companion to MULTI_STAGE_PRODUCTION_BATCHES_20261122.md; no override of canon or adoption gates.
- Extract original pixels from authenticated artifacts; record path, run, model and image SHA-256. A crop is a derivative; record transform and parent digest. Never substitute a display image for the original.
- Annotate stable landmark IDs and uncertainty on source and measured views separately. Keep manual estimates provisional. Use independent registration anchors, matched pose/projection/camera, reject mirror/view mismatch; uniform scale and translation cannot correct perspective or pose. Ratios are diagnostic, not acceptance.
- Record each target, units, tolerance, actual, residual, measurement uncertainty and source. Unknown depth/topology remains unknown. Body scanline widths need explicit tolerances; hair asymmetry requires multiple views; clothes need seams, UV, overlap and deformation; equipment needs attachment and clearance. World analogues include sections, silhouette, contact and traversal.
- Qualify controls on disposable native copies using two distinct measured settings, same context and verified save/reopen. Estimate local response by finite difference, reject zero response or stale context, clamp step and control range; remeasure after every edit. Preserve verified baseline; rollback and verify on regression or ambiguous mutation. Reassess after two same-cause failures under Causal Retry v3; continue independent safe work. Never infer target settings from calibration alone.
- At batch launch verify dependency blobs and actual SHA-256, lease, budget and checkpoint. Reserve save/reopen/upload time within 360 minutes. Keep per-module target and operation ledgers; execute all ready stages; report scoped blockers and exact resume pointer. No fixed idle waits.
- Candidate implementation: PR #427, engine at 5ecd5157d8b02b3700e46d2fb8427c4004ca7361, tools/production_qa/morwen_blueprint_engine.py. Target compiler (136 provisional targets), registration, local correction, evidence hash checks and stage ledger are candidate code, NOT_RUN until exact-byte tests. Native GUI binding and full batch NOT_RUN. Hash presence alone does not establish registration validity. Prior face trial estimates require independent original-pixel verification before use. Do not merge candidate assets or calibration models on the strength of this playbook.
- Acceptance: real native mutation driven by blueprint residual, matched remeasurement, immutable checkpoint reopening, downstream stages and independent G1–G4. A timeout setting, synthetic test or successful CI alone cannot certify a six-hour production engine.
