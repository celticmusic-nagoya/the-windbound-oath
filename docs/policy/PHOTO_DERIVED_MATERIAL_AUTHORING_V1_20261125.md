# TWO — Photo-derived high-fidelity material authoring v1.0
Director-approved decision: 2026-11-25. Mandatory for all AI agents (ChatGPT, Codex, Claude and successors), human collaborators and automations when authoring or reviewing relevant assets. Applies to Morwen first, all subsequent characters/NPCs, clothing/equipment, and relevant world materials. This is an approved production method, not adoption of any individual asset or license, runtime verification, canon revision, or authorization to spend.

## 1. Objective and source priority
- Preserve original Celtic/Irish fantasy design, approved silhouettes, facial identity, costume patterns, palette and anime/stylized art direction. Use real-world photographic microstructure for fidelity rather than copying photographed designs or making characters photorealistic. Prioritize actual completed assets over repeated isolated material demonstrations.
- Prefer self-authored captures with the necessary rights/consents, CC0/public-domain scans with verified status, and other explicitly licensed commercial-use/derivative game-texture sources. Check each source's actual terms, including attribution, modification, distribution as part of a game and redistribution of source/derived textures. Do not assume a commercial-use license permits standalone texture distribution or public repository commits. Respect privacy, model permissions and third-party rights; avoid identifiable personal details.
- Real apparel/product photographs, macro photography and production breakdowns can serve as visual study references. Direct crop, patch, blend, projection, extraction, or adaptation of photographic pixels or maps is allowed ONLY when the necessary rights and license are established for the intended use. Unknown or incompatible rights: observation only; recreate original geometry/patterns or use verified alternatives. Altering a copyrighted photo or making it unrecognizable does not itself establish permission. Never copy another product's distinctive ornamental design, logo, protected character or branding as the TWO design.
- Record source URL/creator, license and terms evidence, access/version, original byte SHA-256, map/channel lineage, modifications, attribution and allowed distribution in the owning PR or asset manifest. Preserve source originals; no secrets, private references or license-restricted source files in public outputs. No paid acquisition without explicit approval.

## 2. Production workflow
1. Bind exact approved design references, scope/owner, existing editable checkpoint, source/license and relevant D0–D4 target. Use original pattern and measured visible placement; do not invent occluded anatomy or garment structures as canon.
2. Acquire legal photo/scan data at sufficient resolution. Prefer calibrated PBR scans; for single photographs, mark inverse-lighting/normal/roughness estimates as estimates. Inspect scale, perspective, seams and camera illumination. Remove directional lighting, highlights, shadows and unwanted photographic color cast from surface Base Color as practicable; never mislabel painted/photo appearance as measured intrinsic albedo. Separate AO/lightmaps where justified and supported; do not bake fixed directional specular into albedo. Preserve meaningful actual pigment, intentional stylized markings and material texture.
3. Reconstruct or locally paste and blend licensed microstructure into original authored motifs, preserve verified UV and protected alpha/non-target regions, align weave/fiber orientation, physical or normalized scale, seams and repeat. Prefer nondestructive layers and reproducible scripts where useful. Separate sRGB Base Color from linear Normal, Roughness, Metallic, opacity/height and other supported maps. Use actual scene light/view/environment for highlights, reflections and shadows; material parameters describe response, not fixed lighting.
4. Compare actual matched native/model previews before and after; inspect close and intended gameplay distances, scene-light variation, seams, aliasing, clipping, expression/deformation and texture budgets. Keep existing VRoid-first rule, native source checkpoints, qualified Blender transfer and licensed tools. Unsupported shader features: choose a tested compatible approximation, or record limits; do not silently require expensive effects.
5. Independent G2 art QA compares official design and stylized consistency, G1 verifies provenance/rights, G3 verifies actual UV/maps/native reopen, G4 verifies game/WebGL/Android appearance and performance as applicable. Record per-gate status and evidence; successful extraction or CPU swatch is not model or runtime PASS. If a source cannot legally or technically be used, switch to a qualified alternative or original reconstruction and continue independent work. Preserve G1–G4 and explicit final adoption gates.

## 3. Module application
| Module | Photo-derived inputs | Required restraint |
|---|---|---|
| Fabric and embroidery | Actual yarn, weave, knit, stitch direction, metallic-thread microrelief, fabric roughness; licensed scan/photo patches reprojected onto original embroidery masks | No foreign motif, brand, baked reflection or invented seam; retain canonical colors, geometry and trim |
| Leather, metal, glass, jewelry | Grain, finish, wear scale, normal/roughness and plausible optical response | Do not confuse material color with photographed lighting; retain canonical silhouette and design; transparency/refraction only if supported and verified |
| Skin and lips | Consented/licensed generic macro microstructure, subtle pore/lip detail, restrained regional color and roughness | Preserve stylized face, smoothness and expression; no recognizable donor face, excessive pores, unnecessary blemishes or baked lighting; maps do not redefine anatomy |
| Hair | Fiber direction, density, restrained color variation, normal/roughness and optional supported anisotropy | No fixed painted photo highlight streaks; lighting response comes from map lights; prioritize silhouette/strand groups; defer extra shine tuning until actual scene integration |
| Eyes | Licensed generic iris microstructure, radial detail and subtle color variation; separate cornea/iris/sclera where supported | Preserve canonical iris color, pupil shape and anime proportions; avoid donor identity, excessive veins and fixed photographic catchlights; use dynamic supported reflections |
| World materials | Licensed photos/scans of stone, wood, soil, plants, masonry and relevant surfaces | Preserve authored world design, coherent scale and seams; no arbitrary copying, false PBR measurements or redundant tests |
