# C7 preproduction observation sheet

Primary visual target is the approved sunset CG (`db0b1f6ae12892047c0def8af53c2bab54b294b76afc08565bf1670252d94a07`). Land panoramas, oblique coastal photos and near-water photos were reviewed through browser search previews. The web reader retrieved the two geological references. Direct environment downloads returned 403. Photo focal lengths were not independently established. Photo permissions were not established for asset reuse, so these are viewing references only. No photo pixels, scans, or textures were imported.
